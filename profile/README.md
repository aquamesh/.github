# AquaMesh

**Operational AI for industrial water.** AquaMesh reads the history a plant already records in its historian and SCADA, finds what the plant is losing in product, energy and chemicals, prices it on the plant's own tariff, and gives operators a ranked list of changes. It is advisory by default: it recommends, operators decide. Where existing instruments leave a gap, the AquaSpectra probe adds continuous in-situ spectral measurement.

AquaMesh began as sensor research at UC San Diego in 2020 and is based in San Diego, California.
